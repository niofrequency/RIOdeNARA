Vision & Mission

Not just a café. A workshop for Papuan entrepreneurs, run in public.

Every table, every cup of coffee, and every band on our stage is part of the same idea: a working café and lounge that doubles as a training ground, where the people who run the room are learning to run their own. RiO de NARA is the flagship of a wider House of Creativity for Papuan small entrepreneurs — a project growing, unit by unit, into its own community of independent businesses.

"Melahirkan Pengusaha Papua, Satu Cangkir demi Satu Cangkir."
Empowering Papuan entrepreneurs, one cup at a time.
